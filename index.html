Hall invoicing

A private tool used by Ananda Ventures Ltd, which runs St Johns Hall in Romford, to prepare its own monthly hall-hire invoices in its own QuickBooks Online account. It is not available to the public and has no sign-up. Contact: info@anandacentre.co.uk

Connect, reconnect and disconnect

Connect or reconnect: the connection to QuickBooks is made by the hall's owner only. To reconnect, email info@anandacentre.co.uk.

Disconnect: the owner can disconnect at any time in QuickBooks Online (Apps / connected apps) or by emailing info@anandacentre.co.uk. Disconnecting stops all access immediately.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Hall invoicing is operated by Ananda Ventures Ltd for its own internal use. It connects only to the company's own QuickBooks Online, Google Calendar and Gmail accounts.

What the tool accesses

  • QuickBooks Online: the company's customer list and products/services. It creates invoices; it does not send invoices or take payments.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): the hall's booking calendar, to count sessions in a month.
  • Gmail: it creates draft emails only. It does not send email or read existing mail.

What is stored

Authorisation tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the company's private GitHub repository. No customer data is copied to any other database; invoice details exist only in QuickBooks and in draft emails in the company's own Gmail.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data. Data is processed only by Intuit (QuickBooks), Google (Calendar, Gmail) and GitHub (which runs the scheduled job), under their own terms.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the company's own accounts under its normal retention. Disconnecting the tool in QuickBooks or Google removes its access immediately. Questions: info@anandacentre.co.uk.
